Privacy Policy

Lesson Player has no accounts and doesn’t collect personal information on its own servers. Here is everything that happens with data when you use it.

YouTube API Services

Lesson Player uses YouTube API Services (the YouTube embedded player and IFrame Player API) to play videos. Videos load in YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). When you watch a video, YouTube and Google may collect information and place cookies or similar technology on your device, as described in the Google Privacy Policy (www.google.com/policies/privacy).

Information stored on your device

To show your “Recently watched” list, Lesson Player stores the video ID, title, channel name and date of up to 8 videos in your browser’s local storage. This data stays on your device, isn’t sent to us, and is automatically deleted after 30 days. You can delete it at any time with the Clear history button, or by clearing your browser’s site data.

Other services

  • noembed.com receives the link of the video you open, to look up its title and channel name.
  • Video thumbnails load from YouTube’s image servers (i.ytimg.com).
  • Vercel, which hosts this website, may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and request times, to operate and secure the site.

Sharing

Lesson Player doesn’t sell, rent or share your information with anyone, apart from the services above that are needed to play videos and run the site.
