BeamNG.drive → Rigs of Rods · physics converter

JBeam → .truck

Turn a BeamNG car mod into a Rigs of Rods vehicle: nodes, beams, suspension, steering, wheels and engine. Every conversion is drop-tested in a 2000 Hz physics simulation before you download it. Your files never leave this browser.

1

Choose the BeamNG mod

The mod's .zip, or its unpacked folder.

2

Add BeamNG's shared parts (optional)

Optional if you don't have BeamNG: borrowed tires and wheels are rebuilt from their names. If you do have it, add BeamNG.drive/content/vehicles/common.zip. If the mod is a new configuration for a car that came with the game, add that car's zip too. Only the physics files inside are read, so large zips are fine.

3

Pick the car and convert

Each .pc configuration is a different build of the car (engine, suspension, tires).

Converting…

What carries over: the soft-body structure with BeamNG's node masses and beam stiffness, damping and deformation; springs and dampers; wheel size, mass and grip; brakes; steering; the engine torque curve, gears and final drive; and the 3D model (body, interior, engine bay) with its colour textures and paint, bending with the body when it crashes. You don't need BeamNG installed for most mods: tires and wheels the mod borrows from the game are rebuilt from their part names.

What doesn't (yet): the wheel and rim models (RoR draws its own wheels), shine and bump maps, gauge needles and the steering wheel, working lights, sounds, scripted features (ABS, ESC, electrics) and anti-roll bars. Convert only mods whose license lets you.
