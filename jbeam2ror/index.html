JBeam → .truck

Turn a BeamNG car mod into a Rigs of Rods vehicle: nodes, beams, suspension, steering, wheels and engine. Every conversion is drop-tested in a 2000 Hz physics simulation before you download it. Your files never leave this browser.

1

Choose the BeamNG mod

The mod's .zip, or its unpacked folder.

2

Add BeamNG's shared parts (usually needed)

Most mods borrow tires, wheels and engines from the game's own files. Add BeamNG.drive/content/vehicles/common.zip. If the mod is a new configuration for a car that came with the game, add that car's zip too. Only the physics files inside are read, so large zips are fine.

3

Pick the car and convert

Each .pc configuration is a different build of the car (engine, suspension, tires).

Converting…

What carries over: the soft-body structure with BeamNG's node masses and beam stiffness, damping and deformation; springs and dampers; collision triangles; wheel size, mass and grip; brakes; steering direction (worked out from the steering geometry); and the engine torque curve, gears and final drive.

What doesn't: the 3D model (the body is drawn flat from the collision triangles), textures, lights, sounds, scripted features (ABS, ESC, electrics) and anti-roll bars. Convert only mods whose license lets you.
